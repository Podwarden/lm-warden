Privacy and cookies.

This website is served by LM Warden, self-hosted by the operator of this site. It has no accounts, no forms and no comments, so the only personal data it can touch is what a visit itself reveals.

What is measured

The operator of this site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people read.{{privacy_ads}} Three actions are recorded: copying the install command, and following a link to GitHub or to PodWarden.

When you are asked first

On your first visit a banner asks whether you allow analytics cookies. Until you choose, and if you decline, Google’s tag runs in its consent mode: it sets no cookies and sends Google only anonymous, cookieless signals that cannot identify you across visits. If you accept, Google sets its analytics cookies (named _ga and similar), which last up to two years.

Changing your mind

Use cookie settings to see the banner again, or clear this site’s data in your browser. Your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage under lmw-consent.

Who processes the data

Google LLC processes the measurement data under its own terms; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.{{privacy_contact}}

The LM Warden software itself sends nothing to its authors or to Google. Only this public website, where its operator has entered a tracking ID, loads Google’s tag.
